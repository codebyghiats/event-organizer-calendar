# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Student Council (OSIS/MPK) members who propose and manage school events, and school advisors/teachers (Pembina) who review, approve, or reject event proposals and oversee the master school calendar.

## Product Purpose
A collaborative school organization planner and event proposal management system that coordinates scheduling and approvals between student organizations and school advisors, serving as a single source of truth for the school's calendar.

## Brand Personality
Academic, professional, and trustworthy. Features structured grids, clear visual hierarchies, and a formal but clean design resembling a high-quality official portal.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic warm-cream/beige "SaaS boilerplate" templates, complex modern "hype" visual effects (like excessive glassmorphism, glowing borders, or neon accents), and overly playful/childish interfaces.

## Design Principles
1. **Grid-Based Efficiency**: Layouts should prioritize clean alignments, clear borders, and structured information grids inspired by Google Calendar and Apple Calendar.
2. **Ad-Hoc Contrast & Legibility**: High legibility is crucial for busy teachers and advisors reviewing proposals. Body contrast must exceed minimums, and typography must be clean and highly readable.
3. **Intentional Event Encoding**: Colors should serve functional meanings (e.g., proposal status, event categories) rather than decorative ones, ensuring the user can scan the calendar instantly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Support high-contrast elements and hybrid/dark mode capability to reduce eye strain during late-night reviews by teachers. Use clean focus indicators and semantic HTML for easy navigation.
